The Society

Where hustle meets style.

Founded by Sleepy Badways, Badwayz Society started as original hand-drawn artwork long before AI tools existed. Re-established in Colorado Springs in 2022, it's now a multi-city creative hub. It's an underground apparel brand and an entertainment production society in one.

Sleepy is a Versatility 3 champion, a comedian, a performer, and a talent curator. The Society brings that same energy to every stage and every stitch.
